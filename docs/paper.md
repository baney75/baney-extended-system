# Paper

Paper gets the same four rooms as everything else, with one tray and one pen.

1. **One tray.** Every piece of paper that enters the house goes in it: mail, handouts, receipts, notes.
2. **Mark the corner** of anything worth keeping, in the top right:

   | Mark | Room |
   | --- | --- |
   | `>` | Do |
   | `<` | Keep |
   | `=` | Know |
   | `x` | Let Go |

   An unmarked page is undecided. Leave it in the tray.
3. **Scan the tray when it fills.** Point the scanner (or a phone scanning app) at `0 Inbox` on any BES drive. `bes sort` dates each scan from its file name (`SCN_20261121_1650.pdf` becomes `2026-11-21 Scan 1650.pdf`) and files it by the words it can read.
4. **File the paper** in a box with four folders named Do, Keep, Know and Let Go, or recycle it once it is scanned. Keep originals of IDs, titles, deeds and tax forms.

Handwritten notes work the same way. A page of lecture notes marked `=` belongs in Know; a page of ideas for a project you are making marked `>` belongs in Do.
