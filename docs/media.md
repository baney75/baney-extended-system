# Media and citations

Anything you watch or read to learn from goes in **Know**. Anything you made or that records your life goes in **Keep**.

## Citing a video or page

```
bes cite https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VIDEO_ID --subject "Biology"
```

This writes a card to `3 Know/Citations/` with the title, channel or author, date published, date accessed, URL and length, ready-made APA 7, MLA 9 and Chicago citations, and an empty section for timestamped quotes:

```
## Quotes
- 12:41 "the line you want to remember"
```

YouTube details come from `yt-dlp` when it is installed; other pages use the page's own title and metadata. Check every generated citation against the style guide before you submit it.

## Downloaded media

| What | Room |
| --- | --- |
| A lecture or tutorial you saved | `3 Know/<subject>/` |
| A video you shot | `2 Keep/<year>/` |
| A video you are editing | `1 Do/<work>/` |
| A clip you will never watch again | `4 Let Go/` |

Large libraries (movies, music) are not records and do not need the rooms. Leave a library folder as it is and keep BES for the things that make a claim on you.
